Rockstar Vienna, formerly neo Software Produktions GmbH, joined the Rockstar family in January 2003. The announcement was made at the 10 year anniversary party celebrating neo's inception on January 4th 1993.

neo became part of Take-Two Interactive, Inc. in February 2001 and is responsible for some of the biggest console releases in recent history, including the Xbox versions of Max Payne, Max Payne 2, Grand Theft Auto 3 and Grand Theft Auto: Vice City, as well as the PlayStation 2 version of Max Payne 2.

Rockstar Vienna is located in Vienna, Austria in the heart of Europe. The creative team of 100 employees does not hesitate to take on new challenges, may they be technical, conceptual or content wise. As a team we stick together come hell or high water, with the common goal of striving for excellence in every Rockstar Vienna project.
