NEWS=<b>The Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas Soundtrack Double CD will feature a mix of select tracks from San Andreas' many in-game radio stations and is expected to hit shelves November 23rd, courtesy of Interscope Records.  As a bonus the official soundtrack double CD will feature a separate stand-alone DVD containing <i>'The Introduction'</i>, the cinematic prologue to <i>Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas.</i>  <i>'The Introduction'</i> tells the full story of the events leading up to CJs return to San Andreas. Why was he forced to come back? What is he leaving behind and what is he really walking into? This exclusive prequel is not available anywhere else and has been crafted with the highest production standards Rockstar is famous for. Look for some familiar faces making a surprise return.
