The critical word on Midnight Club 3: DUB Edition continues to pour in...

GAMESPY 4.5/5
"Rockstar's latest racer takes the tuner scene to a whole new level.

Rockstar San Diego knows how to make a great racer. Its Midnight Club series has always been an edgy, fast line of racer... and Midnight Club 3: DUB Edition is the best yet. The import tuner-style of racing is the theme here, and it's safe to say that DUB nails this better than... those other would-be tuner games.

The robust online multiplayer game is fleshed out with new modes of play, flawless eight-player action, stat tracking, new PS2 buddy lists, and a cool user-created club feature. The level of detail and sheer size of the open-ended city environment is a credit to Rockstar's development team. The vehicles are no slouches either, with an intricate level of detail for all the custom parts and upgrades. You really get the sense that Rockstar was very passionate about making a game that looks and plays great.

DUB is one of the finest racers to hit the market in recent memory, and it's certainly the king of the tuner racer scene, without a doubt. It's a blast that'll keep you coming back both online and individually, taking up a good chunk of your precious game-playing time."

IGN 9.2/10, EDITORS' CHOICE AWARD
"...incredibly stylistic, long-lasting, and deep.

Developed in San Diego where the tuner scene is highly visible even to the ignorant, Midnight Club 3: DUB Edition is rooted deep in the mod subculture and never gives up faith or sells out to commercialism. The intertwined message of stellar gameplay and entrenched culture gives the game a feeling of authenticity sorely lacking in similar racers. MC3 is at once the same beast that MC2 was, yet far deeper, broader, and richer in presentation and culture. An arcade game by nature, it builds its core gameplay around intensity.

Midnight Club 3: DUB Edition is an insanely fast, chaotic, and progressive racing game..."

PSM 9.5/10
"Racing games... just don't get any more fun.

...not only has everything that made MC2 so great the superbly-designed open cities, great control, cool gameplay twists, and an amazing sense of speed been improved, but the whole experience is ratcheted up another notch with real cars, customization, and even more clever gameplay additions.

It's not just that MC3 has real cars this time around: it's the number and variety of them; everything from the classic muscle of a '69 Camaro to modern import tuners to SUVs and luxury sedans. Toss in window-rattling choppers along with crazy-fast super-bikes and you have a huge variety of ways to race through the game's bigger, prettier, more lively cities: San Diego, Atlanta, and Detroit.

... with the blistering sense of speed, loads of shortcuts, and 'I can see my house from here' jumps, no game does arcade racing better.

... this is, without a doubt, the single best arcade racer on (the PlayStation®2). Thankfully, it doesn't end just when it starts to get really good. It's a big game that just keeps getting better."

April 5, 2005

Game Informer Online Preview

The folks at Game Informer Online recently got their hands on Midnight Club 3: DUB Edition...

"It's hard to put into the words the sheer amount of customization that's available in the game, and Rockstar manages to pull both the performance and visual features together into one of the best garage' type features seen in a racing game yet.

While so much of the racing experience in Midnight Club 3: DUB Edition is a jaw-dropping good time, it's also important to know that the game is sporting some of the best visuals ever seen in a racing game. Period.

Never before has a game given players such a raw feeling of speed and power and coupled with the innovative and enjoyable race modes and online functionality, it's not hard to wager that Midnight Club 3: DUB Edition may go down as being the best tuner racer on home consoles. They've taken their time and have made us wait, but so far the wait has been entirely worth it.

While many companies have tried, and often failed, to tap into the world of tuner racing, Rockstar manages to get pretty much everything right; intense racing speed and action, tons of cars and customization options, and beautiful visuals. So, even if you're not a devoted gear head, or even a racing fan for that matter, you'll definitely want to look into getting DUB'd come April."
