Overview

Midnight Club brings to the PlayStation 2 the action of high speed driving and adventure through two major cities in the world. The concept behind this game derives from the real world "Midnight Clubs," in which illegal races actually take place on urban streets. Along with the racing action, colorful characters give faces, personalities, and unique driving styles to your opponents.

The core gameplay consists of 39 races, most of which involve high speed maneuvering against a barrage of opponents through completely open city streets. Living life in the insane lane, escaping the law, and accumulating an arsenal of vehicles add to the allure and gravity of road racing. The true openness of the cities, accurate car physics, and responsive controls complete the package.
