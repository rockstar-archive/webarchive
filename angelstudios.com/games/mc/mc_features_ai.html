AI

Advanced AI helps fellow club members, law enforcement, and ambient street urchins seem almost human:

  • All AI vehicles can adaptively traverse narrow streets and bridges, avoid obstacles, and find specific locations.
  • Specialized AI directs cops to detect, pursue, block, and ram your vehicle and the vehicles of your opponents.
  • If you destroy your vehicle, or the cops manage to bring you to a complete stop, an audio clip plays to indicate that you're under arrest...and out of that race.
