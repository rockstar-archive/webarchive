Physics and Graphical Effects

  • Interactive obstacles will tumble realistically when you run into them. Some examples of these obstacles include mailboxes, newspaper stands, lampposts, parking meters, scaffolding, steam pipes, phone booths, and trash cans.
  • Running into pedestrians sends them flying through the air. They'll get back up though.
  • Dynamic lighting includes 3D shadows on vehicles and obstacles, and reflections of lights on wet streets.
  • All collisions, including simultaneous collisions between multiple vehicles and multiple interactive obstacles, are physically based and very realistic.
  • Thousands of 3D objects, including hundreds that are fully interactive, make your attempts to find faster shortcuts through the maze of streets more challenging.
  • Small parts attached to vehicles fly off in collisions and become interactive obstacles.
