Ambient Traffic

From sedans to buses, ambient vehicles go about their business on roads and city streets. You would be wise to avoid these moving obstacles, since hitting them will damage you and slow you down.

Traffic is always obedient to regional traffic laws, even if you are not. The ambient traffic will try to avoid your vehicle. Also, the boroughs and city streets are full of cops that will chase you down and try and stop you by force, if necessary...
