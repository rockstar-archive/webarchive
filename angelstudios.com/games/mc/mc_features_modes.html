Game Modes

The wide variety of game modes gives you lots of choices:

Career Mode

As the core single player game, Career Mode offers a total of 39 waypoint and head-to-head races. In all races, winning is paramount of course.

  • Career Mode is the context in which you can advance, unlock London, and win new vehicles.
  • The objective in each race always includes completing the race in the shortest time, and sometimes includes competing for new vehicles.
  • Each race's challenger has a characteristic type of vehicle, such as a compact hatchback, a low rider, and a muscle car.
  • Exploiting the open environment, swarming with traffic and cops, is part of your challenge.
  • Chasing and being chased, whether by law enforcement or rival racers, adds to the thrill of high speed racing against time.
  • Blocking and ramming opponents is as much of a rush as it is a good strategy.
  • The cities are so vast that most races take place in different neighborhoods.

Career Mode features two major types of races: Waypoint and Head-to-Head:

  • Waypoint Races: Pass all the waypoints in any order, then go for the finish line before your opponents do the same.
  • Head-to-Head Races: At any time while cruising through the city, you can call another member of the Midnight Club and challenge him or her to a Head-to-Head race. Beating opponents earns their cars.

Arcade Mode

Arcade Mode includes all of the Waypoint Races and Head-to-Head Races from Career Mode, plus an additional 10 races to choose from.

  • Waypoint and Head-to-Head Races: These races in Arcade Mode are the same as in Career Mode, but you pick races from a menu instead of by following a Hook Man. When you play the game for the first time, certain races and vehicles will already be unlocked in Arcade Mode.
  • Capture the Flag: At the start of this single and multiplayer game, a flag appears on the map. When you reach it, the drop-off point appears. Your goal is to get to the flag before your opponents, and then drop it off before your opponents steal it from you. After delivering the first flag, another appears and the game continues, until a pre-specified number of flags have been delivered or time runs out.
  • Cruise Tour: Arcade Mode also includes a Cruise Tour, in which you can freely explore any unlocked city. No time limit, race tension, or career progression interferes with your wanderlust. Even if you destroy your vehicle, it regenerates quickly.
