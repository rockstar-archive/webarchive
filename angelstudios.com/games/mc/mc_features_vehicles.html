Vehicles and Player Control

Eight major classes of vehicles, with many styles in each class, provide over 30 different options:

  • NYC classics include modified versions of some of the all-time popular models, such as a Japanese hatchback, the classic Low Rider, a light pickup, and an American muscle car.
  • London vehicles feature familiar European models, such as a rally car that's somewhere between an Escort and a Mini Cooper, a luxury car hybrid resembling a BMW, and the ultimate dream car much like Ferrari's best.
  • Each class of vehicle has distinguishing properties (mass, suspension compliance, etc.), performance (top speed, acceleration, etc.), and durability. Matching your choice of vehicle to the conditions and opponent of each race is a key strategic decision.
  • The controls balance physically-based realism with ease of use, giving responsive feedback for driving on a variety of surfaces, from smooth main avenues to grassy parks. The streets even become slicker when it rains.
