Levels

Midnight Club features two globally famous but very different cities in which all the races take place: New York and London. These cities have been designed to strongly resemble the actual cities upon which they're based: Landmarks, building types, ambient vehicles, and even pedestrians match the look and feel of the city in which they reside.

  • New York is the largest city in the United States. It buzzes night and day with the energy of millions of people working, buying, selling, playing, and rushing. Some of the detailed neighborhoods in the game include Harlem, Midtown, Central Park, Wall Street, and the Upper West and East Sides.
  • London features many of the more famous landmarks and locations that have made it instantly recognizable—including Big Ben, London Bridge, and some famous streets. Like the real thing, the streets are narrow, winding, and challenging.
